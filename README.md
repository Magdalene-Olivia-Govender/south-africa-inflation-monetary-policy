# south-africa-inflation-monetary-policy
An empirical study of South African inflation, monetary policy and exchange-rate movements.
# South African Inflation & Monetary Policy Dashboard

## Research question
How have inflation, the repo rate and the exchange rate evolved together in South Africa, and what patterns are observable around major inflation episodes?

## Objective
To investigate relationships between inflation, monetary policy and exchange-rate movements in South Africa using economic time-series data.

## Data sources
- Statistics South Africa (Stats SA)
- South African Reserve Bank (SARB)
- Bureau for Economic Research (BER)

## Methodology
- Data cleaning and variable construction
- Descriptive statistics and time-series visualisation
- Correlation analysis
- Regression analysis
- Residual examination
- Sensitivity analysis

## Key findings
To be completed after the analysis.

## Limitations
The analysis identifies statistical relationships and patterns. Correlation and regression alone do not establish causation.
